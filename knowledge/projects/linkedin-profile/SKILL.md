# LinkedIn Profile

## Recorded work
Past work used user-provided profile material for LinkedIn completion and export-focused positioning. Contact and education claims needed confirmation. This Skill omits personal identifiers and does not assert the current profile state.

## Workflow
1. Inspect current LinkedIn fields and latest user-approved profile source.
2. Separate confirmed employer, dates, role, school and contact from positioning copy.
3. Draft concise English for overseas buyers; add Chinese review when requested. Keep brand spelling PioneerGuard.
4. Never invent degrees, titles, ownership, tenure, customers, certification or contact details. Leave unknowns blank or pending.
5. Save only when authorized for that profile update. Reopen and verify each field; drafted copy is not a saved change.
6. Keep personal email, phone, messages and account screenshots out of public files.

Record the profile URL and changed fields only when safe to share; distinguish drafted, saved, visible and independently verified.
