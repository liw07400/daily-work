# Market Research

## Recorded work
Past work covered export-market sizing, competitor comparison and buyer prospecting. Historical direction prioritized Southeast Asia before later expansion to the Middle East; this is a planning preference, not a market result. A proposed 50-prospect Malaysia list was not evidenced as delivered. Some country ranking work lacked a verified complete dataset.

## Workflow
1. Define decision, geography, buyer type, product, date range and acceptable evidence.
2. Record public source URL, publisher, access date, currency, units and whether each figure is observed, estimated or quoted.
3. Normalize competitor offers by material, thickness, finish, dimensions, warranty, trade term, MOQ and date; keep listed and confirmed transaction prices separate.
4. Build prospects only from source-backed company pages. Record company, URL, buyer type, country and product-fit evidence; never fabricate contacts or intent.
5. Score with transparent criteria; candidate prospect is not automatically a qualified lead/customer.
6. Recheck volatile prices, regulation, platform features and company status before use.
7. State method, sample limits, excluded data and remaining gaps; do not present a small sample as a market census.
