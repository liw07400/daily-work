# Content + Video

## Recorded work
Past work produced copy, content-calendar and visual/video iterations, including product footage, title/outro assets and bilingual materials. Reviews rejected unstructured clips, severe crop and static-logo zoom passed off as 3D animation. Some download/model/authorization blockers left requested processing unverified.

## Workflow
1. Confirm platform, buyer, objective, length/aspect ratio, source footage and deliverable; reuse approved assets.
2. Structure: buyer problem → real product/application footage → verified proof → wholesale/OEM/sample CTA.
3. Write natural English; add faithful Chinese review when requested. Do not invent price, test or performance claims.
4. Edit from originals and preserve requested frame/composition. Use approved logo, subtitles and music.
5. Export to requested format. Reopen and inspect beginning/middle/end, audio, subtitles, spelling and crop against source.
6. Record artifact, version, source assets, edit, preview evidence and unresolved issues. A prompt/script is not a rendered video.

A generated factory image does not prove factory ownership. Confirm packaging, certificates and warranty before publication.
