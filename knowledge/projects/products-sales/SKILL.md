# Products + Sales Materials

## Recorded work
Historical work includes editable quote iterations, catalog restructuring, bilingual brochures, product imagery and warranty drafts. Some files remained drafts or needed layout/field checks. A file's existence is not approval for customer use. Historical ordering grouped TPU Covestro, TPU BASF, TPU Lubrizol, PCU, TPH and special films; confirm the latest catalog before applying it.

## Workflow
1. Find the latest approved specification, source sheet, image and document; record version/date.
2. Separate TPU PPF, colored PPF, PVC wrap, TPH, automotive/architectural window film and special film. Do not infer material from an ambiguous acronym/image.
3. Normalize comparisons by material, thickness/unit, finish, adhesive/coating, width × roll length, warranty, MOQ, packaging, lead time, currency and trade terms. Mark unknown commercial fields pending.
4. Distinguish editable assumptions from source facts; preserve the source for copied estimates.
5. Preserve the requested layout; verify ordering, representative pages, crop, tables and bilingual copy after export.
6. Check warranty collateral against the latest approved terms. DRAFT is not an effective warranty.
7. Recheck prices, MOQ, delivery, certification, company identity, factory claims and warranty before external use.

Never invent price, capacity, specs, certification, ownership, lead time, customer results or warranty coverage.
## Customer proof and case-study workflow
1. Split sample tests, first orders, repeat purchases and OEM discussions into distinct stages; do not turn an inquiry or negotiation into a sale.
2. Tie each customer outcome to a dated source record or order evidence before using it externally. Mark user-provided but unverified outcomes pending confirmation.
3. Keep general shipping or warehouse photos separate from a specific case unless the photo is documented as belonging to that order. If a shared reference library appears inside feedback, show a visible note that it is not individually matched to that conversation. Keep original feedback text and source associations; proximity does not establish the pictured customer, country or order. Inventory photo-to-page reuse separately from checking that images load.
4. Remove or obtain permission for names, contact details, faces, vehicle plates and third-party marks before publication.
5. Keep a bilingual copy-to-image map and preview on desktop/mobile; check image loading, captions and claim boundaries before release.

## Catalogue parameters and media — 2026-10-30
- Map each model to its source document/page and units. Check VLT, UV block, IR rejection, thickness and roll format separately; IR rejection is not interchangeable with TSER. Matching the catalogue proves transcription consistency, not laboratory performance or current commercial terms.
- Recheck selected model, quantity and inquiry draft together. A working custom-size inquiry entry does not prove every size is available; a generated WhatsApp message is not a sent or received inquiry.
- Keep film-material photos, vehicle appearance references and illustrations clearly identified. Verify SKU and colour before assigning an image; a colour-matched vehicle is not a measured optical demonstration.
- Preserve a missing-photo placeholder when no reliable match exists. If a hover view enlarges the same source image, label it as a detail crop rather than a second product photograph.
- Record source and usage rights. Third-party reference material remains a local preview until commercial permission or an approved replacement is confirmed.
- The recorded photo reuse rule allows at most two distinct content pages per photo, excluding brand marks. Translations, pagination/filter views, responsive variants and lightboxes of the same page count as one placement. Do not rename or crop duplicates merely to evade the rule; confirm current scope before applying it elsewhere.
- For a catalogue with missing material photos, place those entries after products with verified material images. Use a vehicle photo only when it is matched to the model and label it as an appearance reference; do not present it as a film-material photo or performance proof.
- For a dedicated automotive window-film catalogue, transcribe series, model count, VLT, UV block, IR rejection, units and warranty from the dated source catalogue. Keep series-entry counts distinct from individual models, and catalogue counts distinct from inventory or availability. A filter, selector or prepared WhatsApp draft does not verify stock or prove an inquiry was sent.
- If a model-matched product image is unavailable, label competitor imagery as a visual reference, link its source and verify usage permission before publication. Never present it as PioneerGuard photography. For feedback carousels, use user-supplied customer images first when requested, then clearly labelled installation references; pair an image with a specific quote only when source records establish the match, and verify the final order in the rendered page.
- For a reuse limit across products, audit the image-to-product allocation across every model, including direct links and inactive selections. A visible list or family-only audit cannot establish compliance across all model pages. Group aliases, crops and renamed derivatives by original source/hash, report maximum product assignments and unfilled products, and keep candidate images separate from installed assets. Similar colour or VLT does not establish a genuine film/vehicle pair; never recolour references to manufacture a match. Removing a mark or visible source link does not establish usage rights; preserve provenance privately when it is absent from the page.
- For model-specific detail content, distinguish template coverage from technical-data completeness. Audit each record's own identifiers, explicitly stated attributes, available formats and matched gallery; separately count unknown thickness, construction and warranty. Do not map quotation-series claims into unmatched website SKUs, infer film construction from a range name, or turn catalogue installation choices into universal application instructions.

## Source reconciliation and image roles
- Build a model-to-series-to-source-page-to-original-image index before seeking replacements. Separate model measurement/sample photos, clean material covers and vehicle appearance references. An explicit missing-photo record stays pending; a generic reference cannot supply the absent model evidence. A catalogue image count does not prove the images are installed on the website.
- Reconcile identifiers with names, finish and dated source pages. Same code with different colours, or same colour with different codes, is a conflict/candidate match rather than permission to transfer thickness, adhesive, supplier or warranty. A thickness in a filename and a draft warranty do not establish effective model terms. Maintain series technical descriptions separately from model facts.
- Record current reuse limits by image role and count scope. If the requirement changes from two products per original to one vehicle original per model, rerun all assignments and inspect crops/near-duplicates. Count directory covers, model galleries, the reference collection and the hero separately, remove repeated collection entries when required, and document exclusions. Distinct hashes or URLs alone do not prove different photographic originals, model equivalence or publication rights.
