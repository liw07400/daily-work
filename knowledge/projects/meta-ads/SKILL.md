# Meta Ads

## Recorded work
Past work covered pages, lead forms, WhatsApp ads, copy, campaign preparation and daily performance review. No current campaign state or results are asserted.

## Workflow
1. Confirm exact account, campaign/ad, objective, destination, market, budget, schedule and requested scope from current read-back.
2. Match setup to form, WhatsApp or website. Measure forms and conversations separately.
3. Build English-first copy from approved product evidence. Never invent price, MOQ, certification, warranty, ownership or performance.
4. Validate audience, placement, privacy link, form/destination and tracking; keep new ads paused until launch is authorized.
5. Save and reopen; distinguish draft, review, approval and delivery.
6. Review spend with deduplicated qualified leads, buyer type/country, quotes, samples and orders. Clicks alone are not success.
7. Troubleshoot exact message → exact field → save → reopen → downstream verification; do not change unrelated ads.

Use Ads Manager/Insights for owned-account results. Public ad libraries do not prove exact spend, lead quality, attribution or ROI.
